# Benefits Policy

Policy ID: HR-BN-04 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Eligibility

1.1 Full-time employees are eligible for all benefits from their first day of employment, except where this policy states otherwise.

1.2 Part-time employees receive health insurance in full and other cash benefits pro-rated to their contracted hours.

## 2. Health Insurance

2.1 Private medical insurance covers the employee, their spouse and up to 3 children under the age of 21, at no cost to the employee.

2.2 Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

2.3 Dental treatment is covered up to USD 500 per person per year. Optical costs are covered up to USD 200 per person every 2 years.

## 3. Retirement Savings

3.1 The company matches employee pension contributions up to 5% of base salary.

3.2 The company match begins once the employee has completed the probation period of 3 months.

## 4. Life and Disability Insurance

4.1 Life insurance pays 24 times the employee's monthly base salary.

4.2 Long-term disability insurance pays 60% of base salary once an employee has been continuously absent for 90 days because of illness or injury.

## 5. Learning and Development

5.1 Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,000 for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

5.2 Any single learning expense above USD 300 requires manager approval in advance.

5.3 If an employee leaves within 12 months of completing a company-funded certification that cost more than USD 500, they must repay 50% of its cost.

## 6. Wellbeing

6.1 Employees receive a gym or wellbeing stipend of USD 40 per month, claimed through the expense system.

6.2 The Employee Assistance Program offers up to 6 free, confidential counselling sessions per year through an external provider.

## 7. Employee Referral Program

7.1 A referral bonus of USD 1,500 is paid when a referred candidate is hired and completes 90 days of employment.

7.2 Referrals for roles that report to the referring employee, and referrals of former employees who left less than 12 months ago, are not eligible.
