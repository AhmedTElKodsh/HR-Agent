People Operations FAQ

Page ID: HR-FAQ-09 · Last reviewed: 15 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations

Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

1. Leave and Time Off

1.1 How do I book annual leave?

Submit a request in the HR system under Time Off › New request. Your direct manager approves it (Leave Policy §2.5).

1.2 Can I cancel leave that was already approved?

Yes. Cancel it in the HR system before the first day of leave and the days go back to your balance. Once the leave has started, ask your manager and People Operations.

2. Pay and Payslips

2.1 When is salary paid?

Salary is paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th is a weekend day or a public holiday, salary is paid on the previous working day.

2.2 Where do I find my payslip?

Payslips are published in the HR portal under Pay › Payslips, two working days before payday.

3. Equipment and IT

3.1 Who do I contact about a broken laptop?

Raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk in the IT portal. HR cannot replace equipment.

4. Documents and Letters

4.1 How do I get an employment letter?

Request it in the HR portal under Documents › Employment letter. Letters are issued within 3 working days and state your job title and start date. Your salary is included only if you tick "include salary", and the letter is then sent to you alone.

4.2 How do I update my bank details?

Update them in the HR portal under Profile › Bank details before the 15th of the month for the change to apply to that month's salary.

5. Contacts

5.1 How do I reach People Operations?

Email hr@nilebyte.example or visit the People desk on the 3rd floor of the Cairo office, Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 15:00.
