# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-RW-02 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Scope and Purpose

1.1 This policy explains where and when employees may work. It applies to all employees whose role can be performed away from the office.

1.2 Roles that require on-site presence (for example, office operations and IT hardware support) are excluded.

## 2. Hybrid Working Model

2.1 The standard working model is hybrid: employees work from the office at least 3 days per week and may work remotely for up to 2 days per week.

2.2 Tuesday is the company-wide anchor day. All hybrid employees are expected to work from the office every Tuesday.

2.3 Fully remote arrangements are exceptional. They require approval from the department VP and HR and are reviewed every 6 months.

2.4 New employees work from the office every working day during their first 4 weeks, to support onboarding.

## 3. Working Hours

3.1 Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Employees should be available for meetings and messages during core hours.

3.2 Outside core hours, employees may choose their start and finish times, as long as they complete their contracted hours.

## 4. Working From Abroad

4.1 Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. This requires approval from the direct manager and HR, requested at least 3 weeks in advance.

4.2 Working from abroad is not permitted in countries where the company has legal, tax or sanctions restrictions. HR Legal maintains the list of restricted countries.

4.3 While working from abroad, employees must remain available during core hours in their home time zone.

## 5. Equipment and Stipend

5.1 The company provides every employee with a laptop, a monitor and a headset.

5.2 Hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of USD 300. It must be claimed through the expense system within 90 days of joining.

5.3 Fully remote employees are reimbursed for home internet costs up to USD 25 per month. Hybrid employees are not eligible for internet reimbursement.

## 6. Security

6.1 Company data may only be accessed on company-managed devices.

6.2 When using public Wi-Fi, employees must connect through the company VPN.

6.3 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.
