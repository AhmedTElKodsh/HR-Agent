# Health and Safety Policy

Policy ID: HS-01-15 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Facilities and People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Responsibilities

1.1 Every employee must follow safety instructions and report any hazard they notice.

1.2 Each office has at least 2 trained first aiders and 2 fire wardens. Their names are posted at reception.

## 2. Incidents

2.1 All workplace injuries and near misses must be reported in the HR portal within 24 hours.

2.2 HR reports injuries that need hospital treatment to the relevant authority, as required by law.

## 3. Fire Safety

3.1 Fire evacuation drills are held twice a year in every office.

3.2 When the alarm sounds, leave by the nearest exit, do not use the lifts, and go to the assembly point shown on the floor plan.

## 4. Workstations

4.1 New employees complete a workstation self-assessment within their first month, including those who work from home.

4.2 Employees who need ergonomic equipment, such as a chair or a standing desk, can request it through the IT portal. Costs up to USD 250 are covered without further approval.

## 5. Travel Safety

5.1 Employees travelling to a country with a high travel-risk rating must register the trip in the travel portal and complete a safety briefing before departure.
