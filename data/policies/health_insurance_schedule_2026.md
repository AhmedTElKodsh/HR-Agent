## 2. Claims

2.1 Out-of-network claims must be submitted to the insurer within 60 days of treatment, with original receipts.

2.2 Pre-approval requests are answered within 2 working days. Emergencies do not need pre-approval.

2.3 Insurance cards are issued within 10 working days of joining.
