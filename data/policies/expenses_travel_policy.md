# Expenses and Travel Policy

Policy ID: HR-EX-03 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Finance
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. General Principles

1.1 Expenses must be reasonable, necessary and incurred for a clear business purpose.

1.2 Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would their own.

## 2. Submitting Expenses

2.1 Expenses must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of being incurred, with itemised receipts attached.

2.2 Expenses submitted more than 90 days after being incurred will not be reimbursed.

2.3 A receipt is required for any single expense above USD 20.

2.4 Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll.

## 3. Approval Limits

3.1 Expenses up to USD 500 are approved by the direct manager.

3.2 Expenses from USD 500 to USD 2,000 require approval from the direct manager and the department head.

3.3 Expenses above USD 2,000 require approval from the direct manager, the department head and the Finance Director.

## 4. Business Travel

4.1 All business travel must be approved in advance by the direct manager and booked through the company travel portal.

4.2 Flights. Economy class is used for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires approval from a VP.

4.3 Hotels. Nightly hotel limits depend on the destination, as shown in Table 1. High-cost cities are listed in Appendix A.

Table 1: Hotel limits per night

| Destination | Limit per night |
|---|---|
| Standard city | USD 180 |
| High-cost city (Appendix A) | USD 250 |

4.4 Meals. A daily per diem replaces meal receipts. The amounts are shown in Table 2.

Table 2: Meal per diem

| Type of travel | Per diem per day |
|---|---|
| Domestic (within Egypt) | USD 60 |
| International | USD 90 |

4.5 Mileage. Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at USD 0.40 per kilometre.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

5.1 The company does not reimburse alcohol (except pre-approved client entertainment), personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, first-class travel, upgrades for personal convenience, or hotel minibar charges.

## 6. Client Entertainment

6.1 Client entertainment is limited to USD 75 per person. The claim must list the attendees and the business purpose.

## 7. Corporate Cards

7.1 Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel on business more than 4 times per year.

7.2 Corporate cards must never be used for personal purchases.
