# IT Acceptable Use Policy

Policy ID: IT-AU-13 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: IT Security
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Accounts and Passwords

1.1 Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not be reused across systems.

1.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal and remote access.

1.3 Never share your password with anyone, including IT or HR staff. Nilebyte will never ask for your password by email, chat or phone.

## 2. Devices

2.1 Only company-managed devices may access company data (Remote Work Policy §6.1).

2.2 Installing software that is not in the IT software catalogue requires approval from IT Security.

2.3 USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

## 3. AI Tools

3.1 Only AI tools listed as approved in the IT portal may be used with company data (Code of Conduct §6.1).

3.2 Employee personal data and customer data must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools, including browser extensions.

## 4. Monitoring

4.1 Activity on company systems is logged for security purposes. Security logs are kept for 12 months.

4.2 Limited personal use of company devices is allowed if it is lawful and does not affect work.

## 5. Reporting

5.1 Report phishing emails with the "Report phishing" button in the email client.

5.2 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours (Remote Work Policy §6.3).
