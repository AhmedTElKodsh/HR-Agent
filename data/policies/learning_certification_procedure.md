# Learning and Certification Procedure

Procedure ID: HR-LD-12 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations (Learning)
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Using Your Learning Budget

1.1 Your annual learning budget is set in the Benefits Policy (§5.1). This procedure explains how to use it.

1.2 Choose a course, book, conference or certification that relates to your current role or to a development goal agreed with your manager.

1.3 For any single expense above USD 300, get your manager's approval in the HR system before you pay (Benefits Policy §5.2).

1.4 Pay with your own card and claim the cost through the expense system within 30 days, attaching the receipt and proof of completion or attendance.

## 2. Certifications

2.1 Certification exam fees are paid from the learning budget. Each exam may be claimed up to 2 times per calendar year, including resits.

2.2 You receive up to 3 paid days of exam leave per calendar year (Leave Policy §5.3). Book them in the HR system as "Exam leave".

2.3 If a certification cost more than USD 500 and you leave the company within 12 months of completing it, 50% of its cost is deducted from your final pay (Benefits Policy §5.3).

## 3. Conferences

3.1 Conference tickets are paid from the learning budget. Travel and hotel costs are paid from the team travel budget and follow the Expenses and Travel Policy.

3.2 Employees who attend a conference share a short written summary with their team within 2 weeks.
