# Onboarding and Offboarding Policy

Policy ID: HR-OB-07 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Probation

1.1 All new employees complete a probation period of 3 months.

1.2 During probation, either party may end employment with 1 week of notice.

1.3 The manager may extend probation once, by up to 3 months, with HR approval.

## 2. Onboarding

2.1 New employees receive their equipment on their first day.

2.2 Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team.

2.3 Security awareness and Code of Conduct training must be completed within the first 2 weeks.

## 3. Resignation and Notice Periods

3.1 After probation, the notice period is 1 month for individual contributors and 2 months for people managers.

3.2 Resignations must be submitted in writing to the direct manager and HR.

3.3 Annual leave may not be used to shorten the notice period unless the manager approves.

## 4. Exit Process

4.1 Company equipment must be returned within 5 working days after the last working day.

4.2 Final pay, including payment for unused annual leave, is processed in the next regular monthly payroll cycle after the last working day.

4.3 Every leaver is offered an exit interview. Participation is voluntary.

## 5. References

5.1 For external reference requests, HR confirms only the dates of employment and the job title.
