# Employee Privacy Notice

Document ID: DP-PN-16 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Data Protection Officer
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. What We Collect

1.1 We hold the personal data we need to employ you: contact details, bank details, job and pay history, leave and absence records, performance reviews and emergency contacts.

1.2 We hold health information only when you provide it, for example a medical certificate for sick leave (Leave Policy §3.3).

## 2. How Long We Keep It

2.1 Payroll and tax records are kept for 7 years after you leave.

2.2 Your personnel file (contract, reviews and disciplinary records) is kept for 5 years after you leave. Expired warnings are removed from the active file but stay in the archive for this period.

2.3 Recruitment data for unsuccessful candidates is kept for 12 months after the hiring decision.

2.4 Conversations with the HR assistant chatbot are kept for 90 days, with personal data masked in logs.

## 3. Your Rights

3.1 You may request a copy of your personal data. The Data Protection Officer responds within 30 days.

3.2 You may ask us to correct inaccurate data at any time, through the HR portal or by emailing the Data Protection Officer.

## 4. Automated Decisions

4.1 AI tools may help HR answer questions or prioritise work, but no decision about hiring, pay, promotion or dismissal is made by an AI tool without human review (Code of Conduct §6.2).
