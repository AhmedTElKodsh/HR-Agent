# Internal Mobility Policy

Policy ID: HR-IM-17 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Open roles are advertised internally for at least 5 working days before they are advertised externally.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 Employees may apply for an internal role after at least 12 months in their current role.

2.2 Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (Performance Review Policy §5.2) or with an active written warning (Disciplinary and Grievance Policy §2.2 and §2.3) are not eligible.

2.3 Employees may submit up to 2 internal applications per calendar year.

## 3. Process

3.1 Tell your current manager before you apply. Your manager cannot block the application, but may agree a handover period of up to 1 month before the move.

3.2 Internal candidates are interviewed using the same criteria as external candidates.

## 4. Relocation

4.1 Employees who move between the Cairo and Alexandria offices because of an internal move receive a one-time relocation payment of USD 1,500.

4.2 If the employee leaves the company within 12 months of relocating, 50% of the relocation payment must be repaid.
