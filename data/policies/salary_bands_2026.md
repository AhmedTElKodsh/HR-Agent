# Salary Bands 2026

Policy ID: HR-SB-08 · Version: 2026.1 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations (Reward) · Classification: RESTRICTED, HR staff only
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This document sets the monthly base salary range for each job grade. People Operations uses it for offers, promotions and pay reviews.

1.2 Salary bands are confidential under Code of Conduct §5.2. They must not be shared outside People Operations and Payroll.

## 2. Bands

2.1 Table 1 shows the monthly base salary bands in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

Table 1: Monthly base salary bands (EGP)

| Grade | Example roles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Intern, Assistant | 9,000 | 11,000 | 13,000 |
| G2 | Junior Analyst, Junior Developer | 14,000 | 18,000 | 22,000 |
| G3 | Analyst, Developer, Designer | 22,000 | 28,000 | 34,000 |
| G4 | Senior Analyst, Senior Developer | 32,000 | 40,000 | 48,000 |
| G5 | Lead, Product Manager | 45,000 | 55,000 | 65,000 |
| G6 | Manager | 60,000 | 75,000 | 90,000 |
| G7 | Director | 85,000 | 105,000 | 125,000 |
| G8 | Executive | 120,000 | 150,000 | 180,000 |

## 3. Rules

3.1 New hires are normally offered a salary between the minimum and the midpoint of their grade. Offers above the midpoint need approval from the Head of People.

3.2 On promotion, base salary increases by at least 10% or to the minimum of the new grade, whichever is higher.

3.3 When a salary is above the band maximum, the base salary is frozen and merit increases are paid as a one-off bonus until the band catches up.
