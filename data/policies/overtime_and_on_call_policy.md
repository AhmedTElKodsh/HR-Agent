# Overtime and On-Call Policy

Policy ID: HR-OT-14 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Eligibility

1.1 Overtime and on-call pay apply to employees in grades G1 to G4. Employees in grade G5 and above are not paid overtime; they agree time off in lieu with their manager.

1.2 Overtime must be approved in advance by the direct manager. In an emergency, it may be approved afterwards, within 2 working days.

## 2. Overtime Rates

2.1 Approved overtime is paid at the rates in Table 1, based on the employee's hourly base rate.

Table 1: Overtime rates

| When the overtime is worked | Rate |
|---|---|
| Working day, after contracted hours | 1.35 × hourly base rate |
| Weekend day (Friday or Saturday) | 1.7 × hourly base rate |
| Public holiday | 2.0 × hourly base rate, plus a replacement day off (Leave Policy §6.2) |

2.2 Overtime is capped at 10 hours per week and 40 hours per month.

## 3. On-Call

3.1 Employees on an on-call rota receive an allowance for each day on call, whether or not they are called out (Table 2).

Table 2: On-call allowance

| On-call day | Allowance per day |
|---|---|
| Working day (evening and night) | USD 25 |
| Weekend day or public holiday | USD 40 |

3.2 Time spent working after a call-out is paid as overtime under section 2.

3.3 No employee may be on call for more than 7 consecutive days or for more than 2 weekends per month.

## 4. Claims

4.1 Overtime and on-call claims are submitted in the HR system by the 5th of the following month and are paid in that month's payroll.
