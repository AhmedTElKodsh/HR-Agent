# Nilecrest Technologies — Code of Conduct
Version 4.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Legal & People Operations

## 1. Respect and Anti-Harassment
Nilecrest has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying based on gender, religion, ethnicity, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic. Reports can be made to a manager, HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Line.

## 2. Dress Code
There is no formal dress code. Employees should dress in a way that is appropriate for their day: smart casual when meeting clients or attending external events, and casual (including jeans) otherwise.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest
### 3.1 Disclosure
Employees must disclose any outside employment, board membership, or financial interest in a supplier, customer, or competitor to HR within 14 days.

### 3.2 Gifts
Employees may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to 1,000 EGP per year from any single business partner. Anything above this must be declined or declared to the Compliance team.

## 4. Data Protection and Confidentiality
### 4.1 Personal data
Employee and customer personal data may only be accessed for a legitimate business need. Sharing personal data with unapproved external tools, including public AI chatbots, is prohibited.

### 4.2 Approved AI tools
Only AI tools on the Approved Tools List may be used with company data. Confidential or personal data must never be pasted into non-approved tools.

### 4.3 Incidents
Suspected data breaches must be reported to security@nilecrest.example within 24 hours of discovery.

## 5. Use of Company Assets
Company laptops and accounts are for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work or violate this Code.

## 6. Disciplinary Process
Breaches of this Code may lead to a verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, or dismissal, depending on severity. Gross misconduct, such as fraud or violence, may lead to immediate dismissal after investigation.
