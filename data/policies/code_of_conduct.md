# Code of Conduct

Policy ID: HR-CC-06 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Legal and Compliance
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Respectful Workplace

1.1 The company has zero tolerance for harassment, bullying and discrimination on the basis of any protected characteristic, including gender, religion, ethnicity, age, disability and marital status.

1.2 Everyone is expected to treat colleagues, candidates, customers and suppliers with respect.

## 2. Reporting Concerns

2.1 Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics.nilebyte.example.

2.2 Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

2.3 HR acknowledges every report within 2 working days.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

3.1 Employees must disclose outside employment, board positions, or financial interests in suppliers or competitors to HR within 30 days.

3.2 Employees must not directly manage a relative or partner.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

4.1 Employees may accept gifts from suppliers or clients worth up to USD 50. Gifts above this value must be declined or declared to Compliance.

4.2 Cash and cash equivalents, such as gift cards, must never be accepted.

## 5. Confidentiality and Data Protection

5.1 Employee personal data may only be accessed by authorised personnel for legitimate business purposes.

5.2 The salary of any employee is confidential. HR staff and HR systems must not disclose an employee's salary to anyone other than authorised personnel in People Operations and Payroll.

5.3 Suspected data breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.

## 6. Company Assets and AI Tools

6.1 Confidential company, employee or customer data must not be entered into public AI tools that IT has not approved.

6.2 AI-generated content used in customer deliverables or in decisions about people must be reviewed by a human before use.
