# Wellbeing Program 2026

Document ID: HR-WB-10 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations (Wellbeing team)
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. What Is Included

1.1 Gym and wellbeing stipend: USD 50 per month, claimed through the expense system with a receipt.

1.2 Employee Assistance Program: up to 8 free, confidential counselling sessions per year with an external provider.

1.3 Monthly wellbeing webinars on sleep, nutrition and stress. Recordings are posted on the intranet.

## 2. Wellbeing Days

2.1 Every employee receives 1 paid wellbeing day in each half of the calendar year (January to June, and July to December). Unused wellbeing days cannot be carried over.

2.2 Wellbeing days are separate from annual leave and are not deducted from the annual leave balance. Book them in the HR system as "Wellbeing day".

## 3. Mental Health First Aiders

3.1 Each office has at least 2 trained mental health first aiders. Their names are listed on the intranet.
