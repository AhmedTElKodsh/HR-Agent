# Performance Review Policy

Policy ID: HR-PR-05 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## 1. Review Cycle

1.1 Performance reviews run twice a year: a mid-year check-in in July and an annual review in January.

1.2 Employees who joined less than 3 months before a review cycle are not rated in that cycle.

## 2. Rating Scale

2.1 Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: 1 Unsatisfactory, 2 Needs improvement, 3 Meets expectations, 4 Exceeds expectations, 5 Outstanding.

2.2 Department leadership calibrates ratings across teams before they are shared with employees.

## 3. Pay and Bonus

3.1 Merit pay increases are decided in the January cycle and take effect on 1 April.

3.2 Employees rated 1 (Unsatisfactory) are not eligible for a merit increase or an annual bonus.

## 4. Promotions

4.1 Employees are eligible for promotion after at least 12 months in their current role, provided their most recent annual review rating was 4 or 5.

4.2 Managers submit promotion cases, which are reviewed by a promotion committee during the January cycle.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plans

5.1 A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasts 60 days and includes written goals and check-ins every two weeks.

5.2 Employees on a PIP are not eligible for internal transfers or promotion.

## 6. Appeals

6.1 Employees may appeal their rating in writing to HR within 10 working days of receiving it.
