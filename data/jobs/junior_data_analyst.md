# Job Description: Junior Data Analyst

Job ID: JDA-2026-01 · Department: Data & Analytics · Location: Cairo (hybrid)
Company: Nilebyte Solutions (fictional company, dummy data for a portfolio project)

## About the role

You will turn raw product and finance data into reports and dashboards that help teams make decisions. You will work with senior analysts on recurring reporting, ad-hoc analysis and simple experiments.

## What you will do

- Write SQL queries to pull and clean data from our warehouse.
- Analyse data in Python (pandas) and share findings in clear, short write-ups.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau, Power BI or Looker.
- Support A/B test analysis with basic statistics.

## Must have

- SQL: joins, aggregations and window functions.
- Python for data analysis (pandas).
- Experience building dashboards in a BI tool (Tableau, Power BI or Looker).
- At least 1 year of experience in a data or analytics role. Internships count at half weight.

## Nice to have

- Statistics or A/B testing.
- Excel (pivot tables, lookups).
- Exposure to dbt or Airflow.
- A bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (statistics, mathematics, computer science, engineering, economics). Equivalent practical experience is accepted.

## How we assess candidates

Candidates are assessed only on job-related skills and experience, using a fixed rubric. Name, gender, age, date of birth, nationality, marital status, photos, home address, career gaps and university name are not used. Every shortlist is reviewed by a human recruiter; the AI tool only makes recommendations.
