# Nilecrest Technologies — Leave Policy
Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors are covered by their contract terms and are not eligible for paid leave under this policy.

## 2. Annual Leave
### 2.1 Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees with 5 or more years of continuous service receive 25 working days. Entitlement for a calendar year is based on the continuous service completed on 1 January of that year; an employee who reaches 5 years during the year moves to 25 days from the following 1 January. Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their contracted hours.

### 2.2 Accrual for new joiners
Annual leave accrues monthly at 1.75 days per month in the first year. New joiners may not take annual leave during the first 3 months (probation period), except for pre-approved events agreed at the time of hiring.

### 2.3 Carry-over
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Carry-over above 5 days requires written approval from the department head and HR, and is only granted when leave was cancelled for business reasons.

### 2.4 Requesting annual leave
Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for leave of 3 days or more, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter leave. Managers must approve or reject requests within 3 working days. Requests overlapping with a team's declared release freeze may be rejected.

### 2.5 Encashment
Unused leave is not paid out, except on termination of employment, when accrued but untaken days in the current year are paid at the base daily rate.

## 3. Sick Leave
### 3.1 Entitlement
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

### 3.2 Medical certificate
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days, or for sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday.

### 3.3 Notification
Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## 4. Parental Leave
### 4.1 Birthing parent
The birthing parent receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave, which may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected due date.

### 4.2 Non-birthing parent
The non-birthing parent receives 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

### 4.3 Adoption
Primary adoptive parents receive the same 16 weeks as birthing parents.

## 5. Other Leave
### 5.1 Bereavement leave
Employees receive 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and 2 paid days for extended family.

### 5.2 Marriage leave
Employees receive 3 paid days once during their employment for their own marriage.

### 5.3 Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year may be granted at the manager's discretion after annual leave is exhausted.

## 6. Public Holidays
Public holidays follow the official calendar of the employee's country of employment and are not deducted from annual leave. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they receive one day of compensatory leave, to be taken within 60 days.
