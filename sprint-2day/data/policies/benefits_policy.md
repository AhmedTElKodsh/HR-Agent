# Nilecrest Technologies — Benefits Policy
Version 2.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People Operations

## 1. Eligibility
Benefits in this policy apply to full-time employees from their first day of employment unless stated otherwise. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week receive health insurance only.

## 2. Health Insurance
### 2.1 Coverage
Nilecrest provides private health insurance for the employee, their spouse, and up to 3 children under 21. The company pays 100% of the premium for the employee and 75% for dependants.

### 2.2 Dental and vision
Dental and vision are included with an annual limit of 5,000 EGP per insured person (or the equivalent in local currency).

### 2.3 Enrolment
Dependants must be added within 30 days of joining or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption).

## 3. Learning & Development
### 3.1 Annual learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 15,000 EGP (or local equivalent) for courses, certifications, books, and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.

### 3.2 Approval
Spending above 5,000 EGP in a single request requires manager approval. Certifications must be relevant to the employee's current or next role.

### 3.3 Clawback
If an employee resigns within 6 months of a single learning expense above 10,000 EGP, 50% of that expense must be repaid.

## 4. Equipment
### 4.1 Standard equipment
Every employee receives a laptop, which is refreshed every 3 years. Engineers may choose between the standard and the high-performance model.

### 4.2 Home office allowance
Employees on a hybrid or remote arrangement receive a one-time home office allowance of 8,000 EGP (or local equivalent) for a chair, desk, or monitor.

## 5. Wellbeing
### 5.1 Gym allowance
Employees receive a monthly gym or sports allowance of 600 EGP (or local equivalent), paid with salary after submitting a receipt.

### 5.2 Employee Assistance Programme
All employees and their household members have free, confidential access to the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), which offers up to 6 counselling sessions per year.

## 6. Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and passes probation receive a referral bonus of 10,000 EGP. Hiring managers and HR staff are not eligible for referrals into their own team.
