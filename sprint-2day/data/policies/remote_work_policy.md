# Nilecrest Technologies — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
Version 1.4 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: People Operations

## 1. Work Arrangements
Nilecrest offers three arrangements: on-site, hybrid, and fully remote. Each role's default arrangement is set in the job offer. Changing arrangement requires manager and HR approval.

## 2. Hybrid Work
### 2.1 Office days
Hybrid employees work from the office at least 2 days per week. Teams choose one shared "anchor day" on which all team members are in the office.

### 2.2 Core hours
All employees, including remote employees, must be available during core hours of 11:00–15:00 in their team's primary time zone.

## 3. Fully Remote Work
### 3.1 Eligibility
Fully remote work is available only to employees who have passed probation and whose role is marked "remote-eligible". Customer-facing support roles are not remote-eligible.

### 3.2 Location
Remote employees must work from within the country of their employment contract. Nilecrest currently employs people in Egypt and Germany.

## 4. Working from Another Country
### 4.1 Temporary work abroad
Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with at least 15 working days' notice and approval from their manager and HR.

### 4.2 Restrictions
Work abroad is not permitted in countries under company travel restrictions, and employees must not access production customer data from outside their country of employment.

### 4.3 International employees
Employees employed in Germany follow the same 20-day limit, and additionally must comply with German working-time rules, which limit work to 10 hours per day.

## 5. Equipment and Security
Remote and hybrid employees must use company-issued laptops, connect through the company VPN, and never work on confidential data over public Wi-Fi without the VPN. See the Code of Conduct §4 for data protection rules.

## 6. Expenses
Internet costs for remote employees are reimbursed up to 500 EGP per month (or local equivalent). Co-working space costs are not reimbursed unless pre-approved.
