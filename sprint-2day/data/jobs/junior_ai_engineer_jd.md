# Job Description — Junior AI Engineer
Nilecrest Technologies · Cairo (hybrid, 2 office days a week) · Full-time · Band B4

## About the team
The Internal AI team (6 engineers) builds LLM-powered tools for People Operations, Finance and Support. We ship small, measured systems: every assistant has a test set before it has users.

## What you'll do
- Build retrieval-augmented assistants over internal documents, with answers that cite their sources.
- Integrate LLM APIs into Python services, including structured outputs that other systems can rely on.
- Design test sets and metrics, run error analysis, and report what a system gets wrong as clearly as what it gets right.
- Expose services through REST APIs and package them for deployment.
- Work with People Operations on responsible use of AI: privacy, fairness and human review.

## Requirements
- **R1.** Python for applications or data pipelines, with engineering practice (tests, packaging, CI or production use).
- **R2.** Built an application on an LLM API, beyond basic prompting (structured outputs, evaluation or real users).
- **R3.** Embeddings, vector databases and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- **R4.** Evaluating ML or LLM systems: test sets, metrics and error analysis.
- **R5.** Built a REST API (FastAPI, Flask or similar).
- **R6.** Git.
- **R7.** Docker.

## Nice to have
- **N1.** Agents and tool/function calling (LangChain, LangGraph or similar).
- **N2.** Deploying or running a service on a cloud platform.

## How we assess
We assess skills demonstrated in projects and work. We don't require a specific degree, university or number of years of experience, and career breaks are welcome: personal projects count the same as employer projects when they show the same depth.

## Hiring process
1. **CV review.** Assisted by an AI screening tool that matches evidence in your CV to the requirements above. Every outcome is reviewed by a recruiter before any decision, and you can ask for a fully human review.
2. **Technical interview** (60 minutes): a walkthrough of a project you built, and how you'd evaluate it.
3. **Team conversation** (30 minutes).

## What we offer
21 days of annual leave (25 after five years), private health insurance for you and your family, a 15,000 EGP yearly learning budget, a one-time 8,000 EGP home-office allowance for hybrid and remote staff, and a monthly gym allowance. Details are in our Leave and Benefits policies.

## Equal opportunity
Nilecrest hires without regard to gender, religion, ethnicity, age, disability, marital or family status, or any other protected characteristic. Tell us if you need an adjustment at any stage of the process.

*Not assessed by the screening tool:* working proficiency in English and the right to work in Egypt. A recruiter confirms these.
